Game log

    How to play

    Goal

    Score the most points from birds in your Flock, eggs, tucked cards and (advanced) goals. The game ends when a player has 6 birds in their Flock and everyone has had the same number of turns.

    Cards

    Every card is double-sided: a bird on one side and food on the other. Cards stay the way you gained them. Your Reserve holds both kinds face up.

    On your turn

    1. Take one Nest action.
      • Play a bird: pay its food by discarding food cards (any 2 food cards can stand in for 1 food) and pay any egg cost from your Nest or birds. The bird joins the right end of your Flock.
      • Draw 2 cards: take face-up birds from the supply and/or top cards of the food decks, in any mix. If 3 or more food decks show exactly the same food, you may discard those top cards first.
      • Lay up to 3 eggs: each on a different card with room. The Nest holds 3; each bird holds up to its egg limit.
    2. Activate brown powers from left to right. Every power is optional, including on the bird you just played. Green powers are ongoing.
    3. Refill empty bird slots from the food deck beside them.

    Powers

    • Arrow powers (“… to …”): pay the first part to gain the second.
    • Hunt: reveal the bird on the back of a food deck's top card. Under the listed wingspan, tuck it; otherwise discard it.
    • Tuck: slide a card from your Reserve behind the bird. Each tucked card scores 1 point.
    • All players: everyone draws the top card of the food deck you choose. Against Automa, she tucks a card from the discard pile instead.

    Scoring

    Bird points + 1 per egg + 1 per tucked card + 1 per egg on birds matching each goal. Ties go to the player with more cards in their Reserve.

    Automa

    Each Automa turn reveals a card: she gains food cards and tucks supply birds under her Nest, plays a bird when she has enough food cards (returning 1 egg), then lays eggs from right to left. She scores 3 per bird, 3 per Nest egg, 1 per egg on birds, 1 per tucked card, plus goals.

    Keyboard

    Use Tab to move between buttons and selectable cards, and Enter or Space to choose. The current instruction is always at the top of the page.
